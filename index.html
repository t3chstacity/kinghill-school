About King Hill School

A Safe Haven Where Every Child Discovers Their Full Potential

Situated at Mumbi, off Mwihoko Road, Githurai (Kiambu County), King Hill School was established with a singular vision: to cultivate a top-performing academic environment integrated with compassionate, child-centered care.

We believe that physical accessibility is fundamental to quality education. Our campus boasts a completely wheelchair-accessible entrance, accessible parking spaces, and ramp-assisted pathways, ensuring every child and parent experiences dignity and convenience.

100% Inclusive & Accessible

Safe School Transport

Nurturing Pastoral Care

Accessibility & Infrastructure

Built for Universal Access

We believe physical barriers should never limit a child's education or a parent's ability to participate in their growth.

Accessible Parking Lot

Wide, clearly marked wheelchair-accessible parking bays situated directly next to the main school administrative entrance.

Gentle Inclination Ramps

Smooth, slip-resistant ramp accesses to all primary classrooms, administration blocks, dining areas, and restrooms.

Safe & Child-Proof Grounds

Enclosed perimeter wall, 24/7 security officers, soft-impact play zones, and strict visitor verification protocols.
